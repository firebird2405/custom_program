# PetitCalendar (쁘띠캘린더) Privacy Policy

- Applies to: PetitCalendar — a calendar + post-it wall desktop app
- Effective date: 2026-08-19
- This is the English edition of the Korean policy (`PRIVACY.md`). If the two ever differ, the Korean original prevails.

## Summary — zero personal data collected

PetitCalendar does **not collect any personal data.** The number of collected items is zero.
No login, no server, no telemetry. Your schedules and notes live only on your own PC.

## 1. Personal data we collect: none (zero collection)

- PetitCalendar does **not collect** names, email addresses, contact details, or any other personal data.
- There is no login or sign-up feature, and no account server exists.
- We do **not collect** telemetry (usage statistics, error reports, behavioral analytics). There are no ads and no tracking identifiers.
- The app makes zero external network requests while running. Every feature works identically with the internet disconnected.

## 2. Where data is stored — only on your PC

- All data — schedules, post-it notes, decorations (backgrounds, stickers), and settings — is stored
  only in the app's local storage on your PC (localStorage and IndexedDB).
- No one, including the developer, can access this data remotely. No server-side copy exists at all.

## 3. About cloud backup

- The app itself never sends any data to the cloud.
- The backup feature only writes backup files to a **folder you choose yourself.** If that folder happens
  to sync with a cloud service, the syncing is performed by **your own sync client** (e.g., OneDrive,
  Google Drive) under your account and settings. PetitCalendar plays no part in that process and never
  transmits anything directly to any server.

## 4. Third-party sharing and processing: none

- Since no personal data is collected, there is no third-party sharing, no cross-border transfer, and no outsourced processing of any kind.

## 5. Deleting your data

- Because all data exists only on your PC, deleting it in the app's data-management menu — or simply
  uninstalling the app — is all it takes. No server copies remain, so no separate deletion request is needed.

## 6. Contact

- Questions about this privacy policy: uto2405@gmail.com

If this policy ever changes, we will announce it through the app's update notes and this document.
(Current policy: we collect no personal data, and no change that would begin collecting any is planned.)
