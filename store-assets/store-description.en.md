## One-liner

Free, no ads, no sign-in — your desk's cork board, moved to your screen: a cozy offline calendar and sticky-note wall.

## Description

**It's free. No ads, no sign-up, no login, no server.** It works exactly the same with the internet unplugged.

PetitCalendar is a little desktop diary that lives in **one window with two tabs** — a Calendar and a
Sticky-Note Wall. Switch tabs and whatever you were typing is still there; there is only ever one window,
so your desktop never gets crowded.

Pin colorful sticky notes to a cork board, drag them anywhere they feel right, and dress them up
with masking tape, emoji stickers, and polaroid-style photos — all in a warm, handwritten mood.
It brings the cozy, hands-on charm of diary decorating ("dakku") to your PC screen.

The calendar keeps color categories and D-day badges, with Korean public holidays for 2026–2027
built in — see at a glance how many days are left until Mom's birthday or your next trip.
Give a sticky note a date and it shows up on the calendar as a 📌 entry.

**It works completely offline.** Your plans and notes are saved only on your PC and never sent
anywhere. Your little records belong to you — you own your data.

If saved data is ever damaged, the app still opens without crashing and keeps the original as a backup.
Small but dependable — pin today onto your board with PetitCalendar.

**Note:** the app's interface is in Korean. Screenshots are captioned in English.

## Key features

- Sticky-Note Wall: free placement · 5-color palette · checklists · photo notes · multiple boards · note search
- Calendar: month/week views · holidays · lunar dates · repeating events · D-day badges · reminders **while the app is open**
- Decorating: background presets · emoji & photo stickers · masking tape · picture frames
- One window, two tabs: Calendar ↔ Sticky-Note Wall, with your work kept as you switch
- Offline-complete: zero external requests — everything stays on your PC. Free, no ads, no login
