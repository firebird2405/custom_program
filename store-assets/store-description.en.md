# PetitCalendar (쁘띠캘린더) — Store Listing (English)

## One-liner

Your desk's cork board, lovingly moved to your screen — a cozy offline calendar and sticky-note wall.

## Description

PetitCalendar is a little desktop diary made of two snug windows: a Calendar and a Post-it Wall.

Pin colorful sticky notes to a cork board, drag them anywhere they feel right, and dress them up
with masking tape, emoji stickers, and polaroid-style photos — all in a warm, handwritten mood.
It brings the cozy, hands-on charm of diary decorating ("dakku") to your PC screen.

The calendar keeps color categories and D-day badges, with Korean public holidays for 2026–2027
built in — see at a glance how many days are left until Mom's birthday or your next trip.

**It works completely offline.** No sign-up, no login, no server. Your plans and notes are saved
only on your PC and never sent anywhere. Your little records belong to you — you own your data.

It's sturdy, too: open the same app in two windows by mistake and both sides are kept; if saved
data is ever damaged, the app backs up the original safely instead of crashing.

Small but dependable — pin today onto your board with PetitCalendar.

## Key features

- Post-it Wall: free placement · 5-color palette · checklists · photo notes · multiple boards
- Calendar: month/week views · holidays · lunar dates · repeating events · D-day badges · reminders
- Decorating: background presets · emoji & photo stickers · masking tape · picture frames
- Offline-complete: zero external requests — everything stays on your PC, no login
